# Yke222.github.io

Public pages for the apps: privacy policies, terms of use, support and `app-ads.txt`, served by GitHub Pages at <https://yke222.github.io/>.

- `_config.yml`: `contact_email` (shown on every legal page when set) and `policy_effective_date`. Update the date whenever a policy changes.
- `app-ads.txt`: must stay at the site root. Uncomment the Google line after adding the AdMob publisher id.
- `roku-sleep-timer/{en,pt,es}/`: privacy policy, terms of use and support for Roku Sleep Timer. Keep the three languages in sync.
