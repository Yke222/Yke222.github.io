## What the app does

The app sends commands to a Roku device on your local network, such as turning it off right away or when a timer ends. For this to work:

- your phone and your Roku must be on the same network;
- on the Roku, **Settings > System > Advanced system settings > Control by mobile apps > Network access** must be set to **Permissive**;
- for the TV to turn off together with the Roku, the TV must support HDMI-CEC with it turned on.

On iOS the app must stay open with the screen on until the timer ends. On Android the timer works with the app closed, as long as the system allows exact alarms.
